Türkçe

Homezy, bütün ev hayatını tek uygulamada toplar. Moka seni her gün biraz daha tanır: sana göre rutinler, ev işleri, yemek önerileri, 3B ev ve bahçe.

English

Homezy brings your whole home life into one app. Moka, your kakapo companion, gets to know you a little more every day: personal routines, chores, meal ideas, a 3D home and a garden.

  • Draw your home and track chores and routines for every room
  • Wardrobe, fridge and shopping list, with AI photo scanning
  • Daily reminders, streaks and points
  • Android and iOS
